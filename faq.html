Frequently asked questions

What is available in the store right now?

All eight current VYRO guides are available as free PDF downloads while payments are disabled.

Do I need an account to download?

No. Open any product and choose Download PDF. An account is optional and can still be used for the VYRO account area.

What does the cart do?

The cart is a saved list on your device. It helps you keep products together and return to them later.

Will paid products return later?

The project still contains the commerce backend for a future release, but payment checkout is intentionally disabled in the current free mode.

Does VYRO sell an app yet?

No software product has been published. The current collection contains digital PDF guides.
